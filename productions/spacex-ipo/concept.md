Format: `workflows/pre_production.md` §7.1 · Standard: skill `animate-crayon-verse` + `references/wolf_frame_analysis.md`

- **Logline:** How SpaceX sold tiny pieces of itself to the public, briefly broke the world record for the biggest IPO ever, watched its stock rocket up two-thirds in three days and then lose more than half, explained like you're 5. Plus what that tells us about OpenAI and the next mega-IPOs.

## Key metaphors
1. **Pizza = company, slice = share.** IPO = the first time slices are sold to *anyone* on the street, not just the private club. Reused in Ch 4 for dilution (a new slice cut for the Cursor deal makes every slice smaller).
2. **Velvet rope → open doors.** Private market = members-only club (founders, employees, big funds). IPO = doors open onto the public market (the Nasdaq hall).
3. **Rocket = stock price, gravity = the forces pulling it down.** Day-1 pop = liftoff. Lock-ups = a dam of locked suitcases that opens on a calendar date. Valuation = fuel tank sized for Mars.
4. **Launch pad queue** (bookend): the next giants wait in line with tickets labelled "IPO".
